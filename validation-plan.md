# Preliminary Prototype Validation Plan

**Project:** Geoshield BRCG Reinforcement Panel  
**Status:** Planning template; not an approved test procedure

## Objective
Plan a controlled comparison of soil behaviour with and without the proposed panel. A qualified civil/geotechnical engineering supervisor should review and approve the method before testing.

## Before testing
Record:
- Test date and responsible persons
- Soil source and classification, if known
- Soil sample dimensions and mass
- Moisture content and compaction method
- Panel dimensions and layer order
- Actual material types, suppliers, and quantities
- Equipment identification and calibration status
- Loading method and safety controls

## Comparison groups
Where appropriate and approved by the supervisor:
1. Control specimen without the panel
2. Specimen with the proposed panel

Keep soil preparation and test conditions comparable. Determine sample count and loading protocol with the supervisor.

## Measurements to consider
- Applied load and corresponding displacement
- Settlement at predefined intervals
- Visible cracking, slippage, or layer separation
- Moisture conditions
- Specimen condition after the test

## Reporting
Include raw measurements, units, photographs, test conditions, deviations, and limitations. Do not omit unsuccessful tests. Avoid claims that the panel improves performance unless supported by repeatable evidence.

## Safety and limitations
This plan is not a substitute for a laboratory's standard operating procedure or professional engineering approval. Do not install an unvalidated prototype in a live road, embankment, or public infrastructure.

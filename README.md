# Geoshield — BRCG Reinforcement Panel

**Project stage:** Concept and prototype planning  
**Domain:** Civil / Geotechnical Engineering  
**Venture:** Geoshield

## Overview
Geoshield is exploring a sustainable soil-reinforcement panel for road embankments. The proposed BRCG panel combines bentonite, rice husk ash (RHA), coir fibre, and geotextile layers. The concept aims to investigate whether this layered composite can support embankment stability and reduce settlement or deformation.

> **Important:** This is a proposed concept, not a validated construction product. Performance, safety, durability, and cost claims must not be treated as proven until appropriate laboratory testing, engineering review, and field validation are completed.

## Problem
Road embankments built on weak or poorly compacted soils may experience settlement, deformation, pavement distress, and higher maintenance needs. Geoshield is investigating a potentially sustainable reinforcement approach using locally available and recycled materials.

## Proposed approach
1. Document materials and panel layout.
2. Fabricate a small-scale prototype under appropriate supervision.
3. Define a test plan with a qualified geotechnical/civil engineering professional.
4. Compare soil behaviour with and without the panel under controlled conditions.
5. Record observations and measurements transparently.
6. Revise the design based on results before considering a pilot application.

## Materials under consideration
| Material | Proposed role to investigate |
|---|---|
| Bentonite | Mineral component; its water interaction must be assessed for the intended application. |
| Rice husk ash | Agricultural by-product considered as a composite constituent; quality can vary. |
| Coir fibre | Natural fibre reinforcement constituent. |
| Geotextile | Layer/separation component selected to suit the test design. |

These are preliminary design considerations, not confirmed performance claims. Mix proportions and layer configuration must be selected through engineering review and testing.

## Prototype validation plan
Possible measurements, selected with a qualified supervisor:
- Settlement under a defined load
- Deformation during loading
- Moisture response
- Load–displacement behaviour
- Integrity after handling and testing
- Material quantities and cost per panel

Document sample dimensions, soil type, moisture content, compaction procedure, loading method, and limitations. Do not test on live roads or use the panel in public infrastructure without professional approval.
